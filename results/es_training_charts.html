EGGROLL × h1 GSM-LongHorizon · Qwen3-1.7B · horizon-2 and horizon-3 stages

How the ES training runs went, in four charts

Every run below uses low-rank antithetic evolution strategies (population 256, prompt batch 8, σ = 1e-3, learning rate 2e-4, rank 1) on Qwen3-1.7B. The runs differ in the fitness objective (correctness only, or gated on correct-and-formatted), in whether the update is accumulated in an fp32 master copy or added straight to the bf16 weights, and in σ. Held-out numbers are greedy accuracy on h1's test sets, paired against the untrained base.

  • Correctness-only, fp32 master · job 6555687, 600 steps, then stage 3 (horizon 3) from its step 500 model, job 6634348
  • Correctness-only, bf16 · job 6600957, same seed and prompt order, 600 steps
  • Gated (correct AND formatted), bf16 · jobs 6455002 + 6600958, steps 0–899, 2048-token budget
  • Correctness-only, fp32, σ = 3e-4 · job 6555703, lr 6e-5, 600 steps, checkpoints lost
Data: results/train_curves_len2.json, results/train_curves_len3.json, and the held-out result files results/len2_conly_*, results/len3_conly_*, results/len4heldout_* in the eggroll-vllm repo, branch claude/tender-allen-7u04jz. h1 reference values are from the h1 README's main results table (Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, DrGRPO).
